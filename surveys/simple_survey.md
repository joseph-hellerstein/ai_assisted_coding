Here's a sample survey — a **Customer Satisfaction Survey** with 8 questions covering different formats:

1. How did you hear about us? Check all that apply.

[] Social media

[] Friend/family referral

[] Search engine

[] Advertisement

[] Other

**2. Rating scale (Likert)**
How satisfied are you with our product overall?
(1 = Very dissatisfied, 5 = Very satisfied)

**3. Yes/No**
Would you recommend this product to a friend or colleague?

**4. Net Promoter Score (0–10 scale)**
On a scale of 0–10, how likely are you to recommend us to others?

**5. Open-ended (short answer)**
What's one thing we could do to improve your experience?

**6. Ranking**
Rank the following features in order of importance to you (1 = most important):
- Price
- Customer support
- Product quality
- Ease of use

**7. Checkbox (select all that apply)**
Which of the following features have you used?
- Mobile app
- Live chat support
- Loyalty rewards
- Email notifications

**8. Matrix/grid**
Rate your agreement with each statement (Strongly Disagree → Strongly Agree):
- The website was easy to navigate
- Shipping was fast
- Support staff were helpful
